Introduction

In my own words

Hello, I'm Dustin, a BS Information Technology student at STI College Legazpi, expecting to graduate in 2027. I am preparing for a career as a QA Tester. I like finding out why something does not work, and I pay attention to the small details that make software reliable. In our capstone, an eBook Library Management System for Genecom Institute of Science and Technology, I spent long days building features, testing them, and fixing errors with my team until we defended it in November 2026. Outside class, I study web development through The Odin Project, where I built and published my first websites. This portfolio shows my projects, skills, and experience. I am open to internship and entry-level QA opportunities where I can keep learning and contribute carefully.

From my journals

Reflection

On searching for jobs

Not ready yet, but getting there

When I looked at real QA and front-end job postings on LinkedIn and JobStreet, most of them asked for testing and bug-reporting skills, and some asked for SQL, Git, or Jira. It showed me what I already have and what I still need to learn. My closest match was a Junior QA role that welcomes fresh graduates with testing experience from school projects.

On our capstone

Worth the long nights

Building the eBook Library Management System took many long days of coding, testing, and fixing errors. I learned to stay calm under pressure and to focus on the solution, because I did not want to let my groupmates down. Defending it in November 2026 made the sleepless nights worth it.

On becoming a professional

Willing to learn

Being professional does not mean that I have to know everything. It means that I should be willing to learn, ask questions, accept feedback, and take responsibility for my work.
